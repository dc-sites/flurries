FAQ

Questions? We've Got Answers

How do I purchase a pass?
Click "Buy Pass" to chat with us on WhatsApp. After payment confirmation (Rs. 5,000), you'll receive your unique 8-character Pass ID within 24 hours.
How do I download my ticket?
Visit the "Get Pass" page, enter your 8-character Pass ID, and download your personalized QR ticket instantly.
Is the pass transferable?
Passes are non-transferable. Each QR code is uniquely tied to the registered guest's details for security.
What's the dress code?
Smart casual to semi-formal. Winter-themed attire is encouraged — think elegant whites, blues, and silvers.
Can I get a refund?
Refunds are available up to 7 days before the event. Contact us via WhatsApp for refund requests.
